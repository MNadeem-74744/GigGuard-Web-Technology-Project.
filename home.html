How the score works.

Clear rules

Every warning comes from a written rule with a weight. The same offer always gets the same score.

Kept up to date

An admin reviews reports from freelancers and turns real scam patterns into new rules.

Honest limits

A low score means no known warning signs were found. It never means a deal is guaranteed safe.

Questions freelancers ask.

Do I need an account?

No, the quick check on this page is free. An account lets you save reports, tick your checklist and keep evidence in one place.

Is my offer text stored?

Not in the quick check. In your account, analyses are saved only to your own reports and only you can see them.

Can GigGuard guarantee a job is safe?

No. It shows warning signs and what to verify. The final decision is always yours.

What if a client asks me to pay first?

Treat it as a major red flag. Do not send money to get work, and keep every message as proof.
